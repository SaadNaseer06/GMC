01

Testing new models with Gulf buyers before launch

Sector
Automotive
Markets
Dubai and KSA
Work for
Land Rover, Range Rover, Jaguar, Nissan, BMW, Mercedes, Rolls‑Royce, Isuzu

  1. The challenge

    Car makers commit to a model’s design, features and price long before it reaches a showroom. They need to know how buyers in the Gulf will react while there is still time to act on it.

  2. What we did

    Car clinics in Dubai and KSA, where target buyers see the new model in a controlled setting and give structured feedback on it. Alongside the clinics, dealer and showroom performance surveys show how the buying experience holds up.

  3. What it delivered

    Pre-launch insights and strategies for some of the world’s best-known car brands, and a clear read on dealer and showroom performance.

02

Evidence for ministries in Saudi Arabia, the UAE and Qatar

Sector
Government and public sector
Markets
KSA, the UAE and Qatar
Work for
Ministries in KSA, the UAE and Qatar, DEWA, Happiness Centres

  1. The challenge

    Public services are used by citizens, residents and visitors with very different expectations. Ministries need to know where the experience falls short and what would bring people on board.

  2. What we did

    Research projects across a wide range of subjects, each designed around the people involved: pilgrims on the Hajj and Umrah journey, Qatari nationals ahead of the FIFA World Cup 2022, and the customers of DEWA and Happiness Centres.

  3. What it delivered

    Findings used to improve the Hajj and Umrah journey, boost engagement among Qataris for the FIFA World Cup 2022, and drive service improvements at DEWA and Happiness Centres.

Research used

  • Customer satisfaction surveys
  • Qualitative research
  • Quantitative research

03

Finding the next growth step for pizza, coffee and donut brands

Sector
Quick service restaurants
Markets
UAE and the region
Work for
Dunkin’, Pizza, coffee and donut brands

  1. The challenge

    QSR brands compete on thin margins in crowded markets. To grow, they need to know what customers want and are not getting, and where the brand can credibly go next.

  2. What we did

    Deep research into each brand’s customers, including qualitative research for Dunkin’ in the UAE, to pinpoint unmet needs and size up opportunities to expand into new business verticals.

  3. What it delivered

    Insights and recommendations that helped these businesses increase sales and market share.

04

One offer for travellers and residents alike

Sector
Hospitality and travel
Markets
UAE
Work for
Emirates, Etihad Airways, dnata, Hotels and restaurants

  1. The challenge

    Hotels, airlines and tour operators in the UAE serve tourists and residents from dozens of countries at once. One offer has to work for very different tastes.

  2. What we did

    Work with Emirates and Etihad Airways to understand their customers’ needs, guest feedback research for hotels, menu research for restaurants, and experience research for dnata.

  3. What it delivered

    Hotel amenities optimised from guest feedback, menus built for diverse tastes, and dnata tour experiences designed to captivate tourists and residents.

05

From the branch counter to the banking app

Sector
Finance and banking
Work for
Retail banks

  1. The challenge

    Banks need to see their service the way customers do: in the branch, across their wider financial lives, and on the phone in their hand.

  2. What we did

    Mystery shopping to evaluate branch performance, usage and attitude (U&A) studies on consumers’ banking needs, and usability testing of mobile banking apps.

  3. What it delivered

    Data-driven recommendations that help financial institutions improve customer satisfaction and grow.

  • Customer satisfaction surveys
  • Quantitative research
  • Product usability testing
