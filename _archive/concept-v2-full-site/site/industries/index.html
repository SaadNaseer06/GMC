Technology and UX/UI research

In the technology sector, we help tech companies understand their markets and adjust to evolving consumer expectations. We also bring extensive UX/UI research experience, including usability testing and working with designers and product managers to create user-centric mobile applications.

  • Usability testing
  • Mobile applications
  • Designers and product managers

Automotive

We offer research to automotive companies so they stay competitive and innovative before launching new models. We have conducted extensive car clinics in Dubai and KSA with leading brands such as Land Rover, Jaguar, Nissan and BMW, and we have expertise in dealer and showroom performance surveys.

  • Car clinics in Dubai and KSA
  • Land Rover
  • Jaguar
  • Nissan
  • BMW
  • Dealer and showroom surveys

Food and beverage

In food and beverage, a keen understanding of consumer preferences is vital, and we have a strong track record in this field.

  • Consumer preferences
  • Shopper behaviour

Fitness

In fitness, we help brands understand consumers’ exercise routines and how they fit fitness into their daily lives. We have completed numerous projects for gyms, clubs and the development of fitness mobile apps.

  • Gyms
  • Clubs
  • Fitness apps

Healthcare

We help healthcare organisations enhance patient experiences, streamline services and adapt to changing regulatory landscapes. We have extensive experience with sensitive topics such as HIV in diverse African markets, and with multi-country projects on upper respiratory diseases.

  • Patient experience
  • HIV research in African markets
  • Upper respiratory diseases
  • Multi-country studies

Finance and banking

We offer data-driven solutions that help financial institutions improve customer satisfaction and grow. We have run mystery shopping studies to evaluate bank branch performance, usage and attitude (U&A) studies on consumers’ banking needs, and usability testing for mobile banking.

  • Mystery shopping
  • U&A studies
  • Mobile banking usability

Hospitality

Our insights help hospitality businesses tailor their offer to travellers and local customers alike. We have helped hotels optimise amenities based on guest feedback, helped restaurants create menus for diverse tastes, and enabled tour companies like dnata to design experiences that captivate tourists and residents. We have also worked with Emirates and Etihad Airways to understand their customers’ needs.

  • Hotels
  • Restaurants
  • dnata
  • Emirates
  • Etihad Airways

Social research

With a wealth of experience in social research, we have partnered with diverse clients on projects in the social sector, from the realities of marginalised communities to effective STEM curricula. Our focus throughout has been identifying knowledge gaps and providing actionable insights. After all, it is about positive social change, something we personally believe in.

  • Marginalised communities
  • STEM curricula
  • Knowledge gaps

Government and public sector

We have worked on numerous research projects for ministries in Saudi Arabia, the United Arab Emirates and Qatar, across a wide range of subjects: improving the Hajj and Umrah journey, boosting engagement among Qataris for the FIFA World Cup 2022, and driving improvements at DEWA and Happiness Centres, among others.

  • Hajj and Umrah journey
  • FIFA World Cup 2022
  • DEWA
  • Happiness Centres

Quick service restaurants

We have undertaken numerous projects for QSR brands such as pizza parlours, coffee brands and donut brands, gaining deep insight into their customers, pinpointing unmet needs and identifying opportunities to expand into new business verticals. Our insights and recommendations have helped these businesses increase sales and market share.

  • Pizza
  • Coffee
  • Donuts
  • New business verticals
