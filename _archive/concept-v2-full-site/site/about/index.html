How we work

A global team of seasoned research professionals, built around strategic collaborations with the right field partners.

Our innovative path

We are a global team of seasoned research professionals located in various corners of the world. Our business model is built on strategic collaborations with the right field partners, so we can tap into a diverse range of talents and resources. When you work with GMC, your project receives the personal touch it deserves.

Our pledge

We take full ownership of every project we undertake. From inception to completion, our dedication is unwavering. Our priority is to deliver high-quality insights and data that drive your business forward.

Integrity matters

We uphold the highest standards of data integrity. The information we provide is based on genuine responses and surveys. No shortcuts, no compromises: just rigorous, dependable research.

Strategic value

Our expertise goes beyond gathering data. We excel at understanding the core issues facing your business, and we craft strategies and recommendations that are grounded in the insights.

Why choose us

A team of experienced researchers in Egypt, the USA and the UAE, with global experience across Europe, the UK and the entire Middle East, at approximately 30% lower cost than agencies.

Unlocking global opportunities: seamlessly serving clients across time zones

You can be from any part of the world, and we will match your time zone so you don’t have to worry about different time zones at all. That’s why our clients in the UK and Europe like to work with us despite a time difference of around three hours or more.
