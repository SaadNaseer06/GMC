How a project runs

We take full ownership from inception to completion, and stay on as your strategic partner afterwards.

  1. 1

    Brief and research design

    We start from your business question and design the method, sample and questionnaire or discussion guide around it.

  2. 2

    Fieldwork

    Fieldwork runs through our offices and trusted field partners in each market, with the highest standards of data integrity.

  3. 3

    Analysis and reporting

    We analyse every dataset and transcript in detail, down to the differences between consumer segments.

  4. 4

    Recommendations and support

    We craft strategies and recommendations grounded in the insights, with continuous support and guidance after the debrief.
